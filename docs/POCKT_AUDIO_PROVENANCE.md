# POCKT Audio Provenance

Licensing receipts remain in `POCKT_AUDIO_SOURCE/LICENSES_RECEIPTS`. They are gitignored and EAS-ignored. They are not shown in the app UI.

Original purchased files were not modified. Production copies are trimmed/normalized one-shots.

## Opening sonic logo

- Production: `assets/audio/pockt-ident-v94.wav`
- Native: `modules/pockt-timing/ios/Clicks/pockt-ident.wav`
- Design: `pocket-rise` (2.18s). Analog blip rise, quiet wood, waiter bell + deep kick + timpani resolution. No vocal. Peak 0.70.
- Layers: `BlipsandClicks_Synth_Short_Colorful_Blip_01.wav`, `Wood_Block_Fienup_001.wav`, `Blasts_Synth_Drum_Resonant_Hit_01.wav`, `Bell_Waiter_Fienup_001.wav`, `Kick_Deep_Fienup_001.wav`, `Timpani_Hit_Fienup_001.wav`

## In-house / previously generated production clicks

Clicks under `assets/audio/pockt/`, `assets/audio/classic/`, `assets/audio/tools/`, and `assets/sounds/` are POCKT-authored or previously prepared production assets, not wholesale copies of source archives. Native iOS playback uses the matching WAV in `modules/pockt-timing/ios/Clicks/` when present.

## Count samples

**COUNT ASSETS BLOCKED — RECORDINGS REQUIRED**

Recursive inventory of purchased packs found no isolated spoken number one-shots (`ONE`–`EIGHT`) suitable for beat counting. Architecture (`config.voiceCount`) is preserved. Dialogue and attack shouts are not used as counts.

## Source archive exclusion

`POCKT_AUDIO_SOURCE/` is listed in `.gitignore`, `.easignore`, and Metro `blockList`. Only curated production WAVs ship.
