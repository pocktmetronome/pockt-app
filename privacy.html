POCKT Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

POCKT is a musician practice and metronome app designed to work primarily on your device.

Data Collection

POCKT does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. POCKT does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or third-party tracking services.

Microphone

POCKT may request microphone access for the chromatic tuner and optional recording features. Microphone audio is processed on your device and is not uploaded to POCKT servers.

Photos

If you choose to use a photo as a Custom metronome background, POCKT may request access to your photo library. The selected image is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to POCKT servers.

Camera and Flashlight

POCKT may request camera permission solely to control the device flashlight for metronome-related visual features. POCKT does not use this feature to take or store photos or video.

Local App Data

Settings, presets, songs, set lists, custom images, and recordings are stored locally within the app on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

User-Initiated Sharing

POCKT may allow you to share certain information using the iOS share sheet. Sharing occurs only when you choose to do so, and the destination is selected by you.

Accounts and Advertising

POCKT does not currently require a user account and does not display advertising.

Apple Services

Apple may independently collect information related to App Store downloads, purchases, diagnostics, or App Analytics according to Apple's own privacy policies. That information is controlled by Apple and is not collected by POCKT directly.

Data Retention

Because POCKT does not operate a user-data server, there is no server-side personal data account to delete. Data stored within POCKT remains on your device until you remove it, reset the app, or delete the app.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if POCKT's features or privacy practices change. The current version will remain available at this page.
