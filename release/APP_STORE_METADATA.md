## Promotional text
One precision metronome, many ways to feel time. Switch from clean classic instruments to visual and immersive rooms, train your internal clock, build speed, and run a set — without ads or subscriptions.

## Description
POCKT is a metronome built to disappear into the music.

Under every POCKT room is one native timing engine. Choose a clean professional instrument, a physical pendulum, a visual pulse, a circular clock, or a fully immersive environment — your tempo and musical setup stay with you.

CORE
• Fast BPM entry and Tap Tempo
• Flexible meters, subdivisions and per-beat accents
• Curated click sounds with clear downbeats
• Audio, visual and haptic ways to follow time
• Background playback where iOS permits
• Favorites, presets and fast room switching

PRACTICE
• Count-In
• Tempo Ramp
• Space Trainer for click dropouts
• Shift Trainer for offbeat work
• Session Timer
• On-device recorder

STAGE
• Save songs with BPM, meter and sound
• Build ordered setlists
• Big previous/next controls
• Stage Lock and keep-awake

POCKT is free. No ads. No subscription. No paywall. No required account.

## Privacy statement draft
POCKT does not include ad, subscription, or third-party analytics SDKs in this build. Recorder audio, presets, songs, and setlists stay on the device unless the user exports or shares them. Apple may collect App Store Analytics through App Store Connect. Do not answer “Data Not Collected” unless the live privacy questionnaire and the shipping binary both support that answer.

## Accessibility statement draft
POCKT includes VoiceOver labels for Start/Stop, BPM, Tap Tempo, room selection, meter, subdivision, accents, favorite, Stage previous/next, recorder, and the privacy curtain. Beat state is not color-only. Reduce Motion is respected in Heart and other motion-heavy rooms. Do not declare Accessibility Nutrition Label support until the common tasks meet Apple’s published criteria.

## Support-page copy
POCKT is a free metronome. There is no account and no subscription to manage. If the click is silent, check volume, sound selection, and whether a Practice mask or mute accent is active. Background playback follows iOS audio policy.
