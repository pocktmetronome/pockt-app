# POCKT teacher / creator one-sheet

POCKT is a free metronome with no ads, no subscription, and no required account.

One native clock. Classic instruments, visual rooms, and immersive rooms share the same tempo, meter, and accents.

Students can start in one second. Teachers can recommend it without a paywall conversation.

Practice includes count-in, tempo ramp, space trainer, and shift trainer.
Stage includes named songs, setlists, and lockable live controls.

Do not claim a measured microsecond accuracy figure.
Do not ask students for reviews during class.
