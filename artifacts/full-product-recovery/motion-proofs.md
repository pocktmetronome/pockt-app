# Motion proofs

Windows web renders cannot drive the native touch dial, the native musical clock, haptics, or the audio session.

These items are marked **PHYSICAL DEVICE REQUIRED**:

- Dial slow turn, fast turn, and reverse. The web facade can show a rotated scale from a BPM value. It cannot show finger ownership, grab, or detent haptics.
- Pulse first wave against the first audible beat. Static frames show a wave at the origin and overlapping rings. They do not prove the native beat launched them.
- Pendulum over several beat cycles, including throw and rejoin.
- Accent cycling under a finger, and the playback flash returning to the stored accent.
- Shutter and beat/deck switch timing on a finger.
- Record, stop, play, scrub, loop, and clear with a real microphone and speaker.
- Tuner cents sweep from a live pitch, including stale-listener cancellation.
- Play producing sound after tuner exit, recorder exit, background, and route changes.

Static frames in this directory are layout and identity proofs only.
