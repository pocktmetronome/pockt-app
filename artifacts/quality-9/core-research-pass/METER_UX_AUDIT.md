# Meter and subdivision

Common meters are the first controls: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8, 9/8, 12/8.

6/8 shows grouped 3+3. 9/8 shows 3+3+3. 12/8 shows 3+3+3+3. 5/8 offers 2+3 and 3+2. 7/8 offers 2+2+3, 2+3+2, and 3+2+2. Those groupings are existing signature ids. They change accent placement. They do not invent a second tempo.

Custom numerator and denominator stay behind More. The wheels are not the first thing on the sheet.

Subdivision choices are in the same sheet, with the musical captions already used by the app.

While playback is running, both meter and subdivision are queued and committed on the next downbeat. Tempo is not queued that way. Stopping applies a queued structural change immediately so the stopped instrument matches the last choice.
