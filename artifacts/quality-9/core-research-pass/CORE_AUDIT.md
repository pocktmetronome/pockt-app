# Core audit

Starting point: b72e543. This pass changed the product, not only the pictures.

Surface path a new player can use without a manual:

- Tempo: dial, plus/minus, tap, and the BPM display opening exact entry.
- Play and stop stay on the native engine. The control may depress on touch. Musical lights follow the scheduled beat.
- Meter: the common set is 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8, 9/8, 12/8. More opens custom numerator and denominator. 5, 7, and 9 in 8 show grouping chips. 6/8 reads 3+3. 12/8 reads 3+3+3+3.
- Subdivision sits in the same sheet as the meter, labeled in musical words.
- Sound opens one browser: recent, mixes, favorites, and one family at a time. The count family speaks 1–12 on primary beats. Not a flat dump of every file.

While playing, meter and subdivision wait for the next downbeat. Tempo still moves without restarting the clock. Sound changes still call the native path that keeps phase and swaps the buffer for the next click.

Practice count-in, gap bars, and session limits already live on the practice screen. They were not copied onto the metronome face.

What this pass does not pretend to have finished: a newly recorded 40–80 sound library, or a device-proven Bluetooth compensation. Those are called out in the other audits. Exact tempo entry on the primary instrument is limited to 20–400, the same range as the dial.
