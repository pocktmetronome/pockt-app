# Meter grouping

5/4 now has the same kind of choice as 5/8.

- 5/4 and 5/4-32 accent beats 1 and 4 (3+2).
- 5/4-23 accents beats 1 and 3 (2+3).
- 5/8 keeps 3+2 and 2+3.
- 7/8 keeps 2+2+3, 2+3+2, and 3+2+2.

Choosing a meter replaces the accent pattern with that signature's accents. While playing, the change waits for the next downbeat. The meter and subdivision readouts show the current value and a small NEXT line until that downbeat, then the line goes away.
