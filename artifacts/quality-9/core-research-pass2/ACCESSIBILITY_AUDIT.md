# Accessibility

Play announces Play or Stop and exposes selected when running. The hit area extends past the drawn key. Meter and subdivision readouts include the current value and, while a change is waiting, the next value. Their hit area is padded.

BPM, TAP, SOUND, and TUNER already have spoken names on the dial. Accent buttons speak the beat number and the stored level. Reduce Motion remains a proof flag and does not remove the beat index. Beat color is paired with position and the word on the control.

Beat haptics default off for a new install. Dial detents, presses, tap, selectors, shutter, record, and in-tune each go through one haptic map. UI clicks use their own player nodes at a low level and do not reset the musical player.

What still needs a device pass: VoiceOver focus order, real contrast under Increase Contrast, and the feel of each haptic.
