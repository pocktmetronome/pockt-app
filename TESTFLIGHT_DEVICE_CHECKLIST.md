# Diagnostic TestFlight — iPhone checklist

This build is not visual approval. Look at the phone at normal distance. Do not zoom.

1. Launch the app. First impression of the main screen.
2. Compare the 120 screen to the North Star.
3. Turn the Dial slowly through 60, 120, 160, 240, and 400.
4. Look at the face under the fixed 120. Note whether a repair is obvious without zooming.
5. Play, then Stop.
6. Watch the beats cycle.
7. Change accent states.
8. Change meter and subdivision.
9. Tap tempo.
10. Test + and −.
11. Open Options.
12. Open Tuner.
13. Open Sound.
14. Tap each bottom-nav destination.
15. Check that taps land on the controls you see.
16. Check the top and bottom safe areas.
17. Note any visual difference that is obvious without zooming.

Most important: at normal viewing distance, does the app feel like the North Star?
