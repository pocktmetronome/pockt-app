# Dial final truth audit

The neutral face is no longer a left-to-right color blend along the old needle. The needle, its glow, and the printed 120 are removed. The hole is filled by a harmonic extension of the surrounding dark face, so the broad lighting has no boundary of its own. Circular brush grain is then added from the same radius on clean sectors, so the grooves continue through 12 o'clock instead of stopping on a vertical edge.

The printed 120 is a separate overlay. The 1, the 2 taken from the printed 200, and the 0 share one cream ink, `242,234,221`, with the same top-to-bottom shade. Their original per-digit lighting is not kept.

Needle lighting is separate. `needle-shadow.png` is a soft offset of the shaft and rotates with it. `needle-local-120.png` restores only the photographed lighting beside the shaft, and only while the needle is at 120.
